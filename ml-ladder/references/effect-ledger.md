# Cross-Project RL Effect Ledger

One append-only table across projects recording **what lever was pulled, what it actually moved on an immutable referee, at what compute scale, and whether it transfers**.

## Logging rules
- **Log both wins and nulls.** Null/negative results save future projects from repeating dead ends.
- **One row per clean lever change** (controlled against the strongest baseline on the same immutable referee panel and seeds). Do not log micro-tweaks, bugfixes, or confounded multi-lever bundles unless tagged `[confounded]`.
- **Separate `ours` from `external`** (verified primary-source papers or public experiment logs worth carrying into future RL projects).
- Keep cells tight so the whole table stays scannable in seconds.
